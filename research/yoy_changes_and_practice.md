## Q1. What changed each year between FY 2021-22 and FY 2025-26 that a preparer must know?

### Takeaway
The big structural shifts are in FY 2024-25 (new Table 6A1, 7A1/7A2, 8H1, Table 8A re-based on 2B, separate reporting of Rule 37/37A reversals, GSTR-1A flow-in, 9C Tables 7D1/9K2, threshold exemption up to Rs 2 cr via Notification 15/2025-CT) and the late-fee amnesty (Notification 08/2025-CT) for FY 2017-18 to 2022-23. FY 2025-26 forms were enabled on 8 Oct 2026 with due date 31 Dec 2026; I found no reported form changes for FY 2025-26 beyond enablement, and no source on how the 22 Sep 2025 rate changes are handled in GSTR-9.

- 9C self-certification: Taxscan describes GSTR-9C as a "self-certified reconciliation statement" reconciling GSTR-9 with audited financial statements, applicable above Rs 5 cr under Rule 80 — [S] [Taxscan](https://www.taxscan.in/gst/gst-portal-enables-gstr-99c-annual-returns-for-fy-2025-26). ## Q2. What does ICAI recommend for reconciling GSTR-1, 3B, 2B (incl. IMS) and books before filing?

### Takeaway
I could not open ICAI's own guide, so ICAI's recommendations are only known through a one-paragraph news summary. The detailed reconciliation checklist below comes from a CA-firm article and a software vendor, i.e. practitioner guidance, not ICAI. Common thread: reconcile at invoice level, monthly, before the 3B is filed, and lock 8A/ITC after the 30 Nov cut-off.

### Cited Findings
- ICAI GST and Indirect Taxes Committee released a technical guide to help professionals reconcile books with GSTR-9/9C; its summarised contents are overview of GSTR-9, overview of 9C (above Rs 5 cr), key FY 2024-25 changes. No specific reconciliation parameters were reported — [S] [StudyCafe](https://studycafe.in/icai-releases-technical-guide-on-gst-forms-gstr-9-and-9c-for-fy-2024-25-402144.html).
- Practitioner reconciliation list (HNA LLP, CA firm): (1) turnover — audited books vs Table 5A across all GSTINs; (2) 5N vs Table 17 internal check; (3) outward tax — books vs 3B plus DRC-03, including RCM; (4) rate-wise tax workings; (5) GSTR-1 vs 3B; (6) inward ITC books vs 3B, claiming missed ITC through October 2025 3B before 30 Nov; (7) split ITC into prior-year disclosed in current, current-year in current, current-year disclosed in next, with invoice-level match to 2B; (8) closing cash and credit ledger balances books vs portal, all GSTINs; (9) spillover tracker; (10) detect late rectifications after 30 Nov; (11) 9C-side: Table 8C vs Table 13 (differences from imports and RCM excluded from 8C, goods in transit, current-year invoices claimed next year); (12) Table 7E vs Table 9 (9C) optional check; (13) prior-year 8C vs next-year 6A1; (14) 3B Table 4D(1) vs 6H plus 6A1 — [S] [HNA LLP](https://hnallp.com/assets/articles/7ccaa-changes-compliances-in-gstr-9-9c_oct-25_f-1.pdf).
- Best practices there: link workings to source data not manual keying, track ITC claimed/reversed/reclaimed separately, keep the outward register at original values to support amendment entries, expense ITC in books not claimed due to 2B mismatch and chase vendors, prepare an annual GST management report — [S] same.
- Tally's guidance: reconcile GSTR-1 vs 3B and purchase register vs 2B before filing 3B; act in IMS before 2B generation; respond to DRC-01B and DRC-01C; claim missed eligible ITC by the earlier of 30 Nov after year-end or the GSTR-9 filing date; compare auto-populated draft table by table; pay shortfall via DRC-03; keep a working file explaining each difference with invoice numbers — [S] [Tally Solutions](https://tallysolutions.com/gst/what-gstr-9-mismatches-trigger-scrutiny-notices-and-how-to-avoid-them/).
- IMS: users can act until 3B is filed and regenerate 2B; credit notes can be held pending one period — [S] HNA newsletter above.
- GSTN-side tolerance: I found no published numeric tolerance (for example a percentage) from ICAI, GSTN or CBIC. Table 6J should be zero and Table 9 variances need reasons — [S] HNA LLP.

### Inferences
- "Tolerance practice" in the sources is explain-every-difference rather than numeric thresholds; if a numeric tolerance is wanted, it would be an internal firm policy, not one recommended by ICAI/GSTN/CBIC.
- With IMS, the books-vs-2B reconciliation should be done on the final (post-IMS, post-cut-off) 2B; 8A freezes around 1 Dec.

### Gaps
- Actual ICAI guide text, ICAI FAQs and any ICAI "recommended parameters" list: not opened.
- ICAI/idtc.icai.org not reached; a navimumbai.icai.org result (detail/56082) appeared in search but I did not open it.

## Q3. What are the top reconciliation differences that attract notices, and common preparer errors?

### Takeaway
Departmental scrutiny (Instruction 02/2022-GST) is built on 3B vs 1 vs 2A/2B, RCM, ISD, imports, cancelled/non-filing suppliers, section 16(4) timing, reversals, interest and late fee. GSTR-9/9C-specific mismatch triggers are documented only in practitioner articles. Note the official instruction does not mention GSTR-9/9C.

### Cited Findings
**Department parameters [P]** — [CBIC Instruction 02/2022-GST, 22.03.2022](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-06/instruction-no-022022-gst-dated-22032022.pdf), Annexure B (indicative):
- 3B Table 3.1(a)/(b) vs GSTR-1 (higher GSTR-1 liability suggests short payment).
- RCM: 3.1(d) not lower than RCM inward supplies in 2A; RCM ITC 4(A)(2)/(3) not above RCM liability; cash paid for RCM in 6.1 should cover it.
- ISD ITC 4(A)(4) vs 2A Table 7; other ITC 4(A)(5) vs 2A Tables 3 and 5; imports 4(A)(1) vs 2A Tables 10/11 (ICEGATE).
- Taxable value 3.1(a) not below net TCS/TDS credit; tax not below e-way bill tax.
- ITC on invoices after supplier's retrospective cancellation; from non-filers; beyond section 16(4) time; Rule 42/43 reversals in 4(B); section 50 interest; section 47 late fee.
- Process: ASMT-10 (one compiled per GSTIN per year) on department data; reply ASMT-11 in 30 days; ASMT-12 closure; else section 73/74; DRC-03 payments already made are considered.

**GSTR-9 mismatches said to trigger ASMT-10 [S]** — [Tally Solutions](https://tallysolutions.com/gst/what-gstr-9-mismatches-trigger-scrutiny-notices-and-how-to-avoid-them/): GSTR-1 tax above 3B (Tables 4/5 vs 9); 3B ITC above 2B (Table 6 vs 8A, gap in 8D); ITC claimed late (8C, 13); blocked/common ITC not reversed (Table 7); RCM ITC above RCM tax paid (6C/6D/6F vs 4G); import ITC vs customs (6E vs 8G); turnover below books, e-invoices or e-way bills (5N vs financials and 9C); HSN inconsistent with turnover or rates (17, 18); cancelled-supplier ITC (6B); unpaid interest.

**Preparer error list as asked** (treated only where sources support):
- Netting credit/debit notes in Table 4: not permitted from FY 2021-22 on (separate disclosure; netting allowed in Table 5) — [S] HNA LLP.
- Reversals dumped in 7H: no longer permissible for FY 2024-25 since Rule 37/37A have their own rows 7A1/7A2 and all reversals must be shown separately — [S] HNA LLP.
- 6A1 misuse: reclaimed ITC should not be included; department expected to compare prior-year Tables 12/13 with current 6A1 — [S] StudyCafe; HNA LLP.
- 8A vs 8D: 8A is now a static view of 2B for the period; the online 8A is the reference; Excel can differ because of RCM entries, charge-type amendments, POS changes and cross-year amendments — [S] Cashflo (GSTN FAQ summary).
- Cross-year items: FY 2023-24 supplies disclosed in FY 2024-25 3B should not go in Tables 4/5 and show as positive variance in Table 9; credit notes of FY 2023-24 disclosed in FY 2024-25 create a compensating Table 9 variance — [S] HNA LLP.
- 6M (ITC-02) was being used as a balancing cell, now barred; Table 9 positive variances need reasons — [S] HNA LLP.
- Turnover reasons: 9C turnover reconciliation lines 5B-5N must now be individually shown (opening unbilled revenue may merge into 5O) — [S] HNA LLP. This is the structural place for advances and unbilled revenue; no source discussed advances/unbilled revenue treatment in substance.
- Missed claims: ITC missed in the year can be claimed in 3B filed up to 30 Nov (Oct 3B) and is shown in 13; consider tracker for spillover — [S] HNA LLP; Tally.
- Notices based on Table 8 may become less relevant because DRC-01C matches 2B vs 3B in-year (an IDS article cited by HNA) — [S] HNA newsletter. Circular 254/11/2025-GST (27 Oct 2025) assigns proper officers for adjudication under sections 74A, 75(2), 122 by monetary limits — [S] same.

### Inferences
- Since blocked credits (section 17(5)), RCM, ITC reversed and reclaimed, and advances/unbilled revenue have no dedicated source in this pass, practitioner notes on them should be treated as unsupported until sourced; their placement in tables (7, 6C/6D/6F, 6H/6A1, 9C 5C-5N) is inferred from the table descriptions.
- Instruction 02/2022 predates 2B-based scrutiny and DRC-01B/01C; current scrutiny priorities likely emphasise 3B vs 2B/IMS; unverified.

### Gaps
- Any official CBIC or ICAI list of GSTR-9/9C-specific scrutiny parameters; later scrutiny instructions (for example one covering FY 2020-21 onward): not found. A search result "ins-gst-no-02-2023.pdf" on gstcouncil.gov.in was returned but not opened.
- DRC-01 treatment of 9C/9 mismatches (section 73/74/74A), limitation periods: not sourced.
- Section 16(5)/(6) retrospective relief and its annual-return treatment: not covered.
- Blocked-credit and advance/unbilled revenue specifics: no source.

## Q4. What GSTN downloads/dashboards exist (Table 8A document details, Table 17 HSN) and how should they be used?

### Takeaway
GSTN provides (per FAQ summaries) an invoice-wise Excel "Download Table 8A Document Details" for vendor reconciliation, and an Excel of GSTR-1/1A Table 12 HSN details to assist Table 17. Enablement itself is gated on all 1 and 3B returns being filed. I found no source describing a broader GSTN "dashboard" that checks Table 8D or turnover.

### Cited Findings
- 8A document-details download: invoice-wise Excel for reconciling with vendors; online Table 8A prevails if different (RCM entries, charge-type amendments, POS changes, cross-year amendments); amendments by suppliers that move an invoice to FY 2025-26 drop it from FY 2024-25 8A; invoices added Apr-Oct 2025 appear once the recipient files the matching 3B — [S] [Cashflo summary of GSTN FAQ](https://www.cashflo.io/magazine/gstr-9-gstr-9c-faqs-for-fy-2024-25).
- HSN: Excel titled "Table 12 of GSTR-1/1A HSN Details" consolidates outward HSN to help fill Table 17; the 65% concessional tax checkbox was removed from Tables 17 and 18 — [S] Cashflo.
- Enablement: tile appears only when all GSTR-1 and 3B for the year are filed (FY 2025-26: GSTN advisory dated 13 Oct 2026) — [S] [Taxo](https://taxo.online/?p=552066); [S] Taxscan.
- Auto-population scope: Tables 4 and 5 from GSTR-1, IFF and GSTR-1A; Table 6A from 3B; Table 8A from 2B; many tables auto-populated from filed returns — [S] Cashflo; Taxscan.
- Portal flags that sit alongside: DRC-01B (GSTR-1 vs 3B gaps) and DRC-01C (3B ITC above 2B) — [S] Tally Solutions.
- Suggested use (practitioner): compare draft with books table by table; pay shortfall via DRC-03; use 8A Excel to chase vendors — [S] Tally; HNA LLP.

### Inferences
- The 8A Excel and the HSN Excel are reconciliation aids, not authoritative filings; the online table is the reference per the FAQ summary.
- Because 8A freezes around 1 Dec, download the Excel after that date, and keep a dated copy as audit evidence.

### Gaps
- GSTN's own FAQ document and portal screens not opened; no information on whether FY 2025-26 8A cut-off or HSN download differ.
- No source on any GSTN "dashboard" beyond the above; the "tools/dashboards that check" part of the brief remains unanswered, beyond DRC-01B/01C and the table-difference fields (6J, 8I, 9) described in HNA LLP.
- No source on GSTR-2A/2B reconciliation tool (ITC reconciliation statement) or Table 9C "9C dashboard".
