## 1. Exact current thresholds and the notifications backing them

### Takeaway
GSTR-9 is exempt for aggregate turnover up to Rs 2 crore under Notification 15/2025-Central Tax dated 17 Sep 2025 (FY 2024-25 onwards, so it covers FY 2025-26). GSTR-9C is required only above Rs 5 crore and is self-certified (no CA/CMA certification). The notification number and effective FY are consistently reported by multiple secondary sources but not read from the primary text.

### Cited Findings
- Notification No. 15/2025-Central Tax, dated 17 Sep 2025, exempts a registered person with aggregate turnover up to Rs 2 crore in a financial year from filing the annual return in GSTR-9; applies "from the financial year 2024-25 onwards" — [Studycafe](https://studycafe.in/govt-exempts-small-businesses-from-gst-annual-return-filing-read-notification-393480.html); [Lexplosion](https://lexplosion.in/cbic-exempts-taxpayers-with-aggregate-turnover-upto-rs-2-crores-from-filing-gst-annual-return-for-financial-year-2024-25-onwards/); [CAclubindia (12 Oct 2026)](https://www.caclubindia.com/news/gst-portal-enables-gstr-9-and-gstr-9c-filing-for-fy-2025-26-26861.asp)
- Enabling power reported as the first proviso to section 44(1) CGST Act (exemption on GST Council recommendation) — [Studycafe](https://studycafe.in/govt-exempts-small-businesses-from-gst-annual-return-filing-read-notification-393480.html) (secondary; the page did not reproduce the notification text, so confirm against the Gazette)
- Same day (17 Sep 2025) CBIC issued Notifications 13/2025-CT (CGST Third Amendment Rules 2025), 14/2025-CT (category of persons under s.54(6) refunds), 16/2025-CT (commences Finance Act 2025 provisions, ss.121-124, 126-134, from 1 Oct 2025) — [Studycafe](https://studycafe.in/cbic-issues-4-notifications-to-notify-changes-in-rules-related-to-gstr-9-and-gstr-9c-gst-refund-and-more-393770.html); [GSTGyaan list](https://gstgyaan.com/category/gst-laws/notifications/central-tax/2025)
- Tiering for FY 2024-25 and FY 2025-26: up to Rs 2 cr — GSTR-9 not required (exempt) and 9C not required; Rs 2-5 cr — GSTR-9 mandatory, 9C not required; above Rs 5 cr — GSTR-9 plus self-certified 9C — [Oquilia](https://www.oquilia.com/news/gstr-9-9c-annual-return-31-december-watchlist) (FY 2024-25); [SKM Associates](https://www.skmassociates.com/post/gstr-9-annual-return-for-fy-2025-26-due-date-exemptions-compliance-checklist) (FY 2025-26); [Taxscan citing GSTN advisory](https://www.taxscan.in/gst/gst-portal-enables-gstr-99c-annual-returns-for-fy-2025-26)
- Rule 80 CGST Rules is the 9C basis; 9C threshold was Rs 2 cr before 1 Aug 2021 and Rs 5 cr from that date — [CBIC Circular 246/03/2025-GST (primary PDF)](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/cir-cgst-246-03-2025.pdf)
- GSTR-9C is "self-certified" since the 2021 amendment (omission of the CA/CMA audit/certification requirement under s.35(5)); a reconciliation of the annual return with audited financial statements — [SKM Associates](https://www.skmassociates.com/post/gstr-9-annual-return-for-fy-2025-26-due-date-exemptions-compliance-checklist); [Taxscan](https://www.taxscan.in/gst/gst-portal-enables-gstr-99c-annual-returns-for-fy-2025-26)
- Persons said to be outside GSTR-9 regardless of turnover: composition taxpayers (they use GSTR-9A, not GSTR-9, in the secondary source's framing), casual taxable persons, ISDs, NRTPs, and TCS collectors under s.52 — [SKM Associates](https://www.skmassociates.com/post/gstr-9-annual-return-for-fy-2025-26-due-date-exemptions-compliance-checklist) (secondary, no statutory cite given). Note the statutory text of s.44(2)/Rule 80 mentions persons excluded: ISD, TDS/TCS deductors/collectors under s.51/52, casual taxable persons, NRTPs — **[UNVERIFIED]** from background knowledge, not fetched.
- Specific 9C exemptions: foreign airline companies (Notification 09/2020-CT) and OIDAR non-resident suppliers to unregistered persons (Notification 30/2019-CT) — [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/gst-reconciliation-statement-gstr-9c)
- Aggregate turnover meaning: PAN-wise, all-India, across all GSTINs of a PAN (s.2(6) CGST Act) including exempt supplies and exports, excluding taxes and inward supplies on RCM — **[UNVERIFIED]**, background knowledge; no source fetched. The Rs 2 cr / Rs 5 cr tests are applied per GSTIN (each registration is evaluated on the PAN-level aggregate turnover) — **[UNVERIFIED]**, no source located.

### Inferences
- Because Notification 15/2025 says "FY 2024-25 onwards", FY 2025-26 is covered without a new notification; the Rs 2 cr limit remains in force as of Oct 2026 per the Oct 2026 GSTN-advisory coverage.
- Whether the Rs 2 cr test is "up to" (inclusive) is as reported ("up to Rs 2 crore"); one summary says "less than Rs 2 crore" ([Studycafe 4-notifications piece](https://studycafe.in/cbic-issues-4-notifications-to-notify-changes-in-rules-related-to-gstr-9-and-gstr-9c-gst-refund-and-more-393770.html)) — conflict; the majority of sources say "up to".

## 3. Late fee rates and caps (s.47(2)), Circular 246/03/2025, penalty under s.125

### Takeaway
Statutory late fee is Rs 100/day CGST plus Rs 100/day SGST (Rs 200/day) capped at 0.25% + 0.25% (0.5%) of State/UT turnover, but Notification 07/2023-CT reduces it from FY 2022-23: Rs 50/day (25+25) and 0.04% cap for turnover up to Rs 5 cr; Rs 100/day (50+50) and 0.04% cap for Rs 5-20 cr; above Rs 20 cr the full statutory Rs 200/day and 0.5% apply. Late fee is charged once for the complete return (9 + 9C) per Circular 246/03/2025.

### Cited Findings
- s.47(2): Rs 100/day, capped at a quarter per cent of turnover in the State/UT (per CGST; matching SGST) — [TaxGuru (2018 article; quotes statute)](https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/consequences-not-filing-gst-annual-return.html); Rs 200/day, cap 0.5% — [Oquilia](https://www.oquilia.com/news/gstr-9-9c-annual-return-31-december-watchlist); [SKM Associates](https://www.skmassociates.com/post/gstr-9-annual-return-for-fy-2025-26-due-date-exemptions-compliance-checklist) (cap not specified there); [NBA Office](https://www.nbaoffice.com/gstr-9-gstr-9c-fy-2025-26/) says ~0.25% (this appears to be the CGST half only; conflicting presentation)
- Notification 07/2023-CT dated 31 Mar 2023 (under s.128), FY 2022-23 onwards: up to Rs 5 cr — Rs 50/day total (25 CGST + 25 SGST), cap 0.04% (0.02% + 0.02%); Rs 5-20 cr — Rs 100/day (50+50), cap 0.04% (0.02% + 0.02%) — [Taxmann](https://taxmann.com/post/blog/cbic-reduces-late-fees-for-gst-annual-returns-notification); [GST Council PDF](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-05/07_eng.pdf) (fetch summary gave the CGST-half figures: Rs 25/day and 0.02% for up to Rs 5 cr; Rs 50/day and 0.02% for Rs 5-20 cr, consistent with Taxmann's totals). Above Rs 20 cr: no concession, so Rs 200/day and 0.5% (0.25% + 0.25%) — inference; Taxmann and the PDF summary did not address it, and one article (CalcGuru) lists "0.50% above" Rs 20 cr — [CalcGuru](https://calcguru.in/?p=572) (internally inconsistent article)
- Same notification: for FY 2017-18 to 2021-22 returns filed 1 Apr to 30 Jun 2023, late fee above Rs 10,000 waived — [GST Council PDF](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-05/07_eng.pdf)
- Turnover for the cap includes exempt supplies (State/UT turnover) — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/consequences-not-filing-gst-annual-return.html) (2018 article)
- Circular 246/03/2025-GST dated 30 Jan 2025: annual return is complete only when 9 and (if required) 9C are filed; late fee under s.47(2) is levied once, not separately for 9 and 9C; the period runs from the due date to the date of filing the complete return (the later of 9/9C) — [CBIC primary](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/cir-cgst-246-03-2025.pdf)
- Notification 08/2025-CT dated 23 Jan 2025: for FY up to 2022-23, late fee for delay in 9C beyond the 9 filing date waived if 9C filed by 31 Mar 2025; fees already paid not refunded; does not cover late GSTR-9 — [CBIC Circular 246](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/cir-cgst-246-03-2025.pdf); [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/clarification-on-late-fee-for-gstr-9-gstr-9c-filing)
- Table 17-style "Late fee payable and paid" is reported for the forms and fee paid via DRC-03 (as described) — [Oquilia](https://www.oquilia.com/news/gstr-9-9c-annual-return-31-december-watchlist) (author says could not verify); [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/gst-reconciliation-statement-gstr-9c)
- s.125 general penalty: up to Rs 25,000 (CGST) plus Rs 25,000 (SGST) where no specific penalty — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/consequences-not-filing-gst-annual-return.html) (2018 article; the figure was later; check the current section text). Applying s.125 specifically to non-filing of 9C is background knowledge — **[UNVERIFIED]**; Circular 246 treats late fee under s.47(2) as the consequence for delayed 9C.
- Other consequences of non-filing: notice under s.46 giving 15 days — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/consequences-not-filing-gst-annual-return.html)

### Inferences
- For a Rs 2-5 cr taxpayer, FY 2025-26 late fee is Rs 50/day capped at 0.04% of State/UT turnover (e.g., Rs 2 cr turnover gives a Rs 8,000 cap); relies on Notification 07/2023 still applying to FY 2025-26 ("FY 2022-23 onwards") — no later amendment found.
- Note a discrepancy within secondary sources: SKM/Oquilia quote Rs 200/day and 0.5% without the 07/2023 tiering; those figures only apply above Rs 20 cr.

## 4. Section 16(4), amendment time limits, revision, and special cases

### Takeaway
ITC for FY 2025-26 can be availed only up to 30 Nov 2026 or the date of filing the annual return, whichever is earlier (as reported). GSTR-9 cannot be revised once filed (background knowledge, not source-verified). The three-year bar for filing returns (including GSTR-9) applies via Finance Act 2023 amendments.

### Cited Findings
- s.16(4): ITC for FY 2025-26 by 30 Nov 2026 or annual return filing date, whichever earlier — [A2Z Taxcorp](https://a2ztaxcorp.net/gstr-9-gstr-9c-filing-enabled-for-fy-2025-26-on-gst-portal/); [NBA Office](https://www.nbaoffice.com/gstr-9-gstr-9c-fy-2025-26/). Same rule for FY 2024-25: 30 Nov 2025 — [Oquilia](https://www.oquilia.com/news/gstr-9-9c-annual-return-31-december-watchlist)
- Three-year bar (Finance Act 2023, ss.37, 39, 44, 52) on filing returns after three years from due date; portal enforcement from 1 Aug 2025 (GSTR-9/9C for FY 2020-21 barred); cited Notification 28/2023-CT dated 31 Jul 2023 — [TaxGuru on GSTN advisory](https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/gstn-advisory-file-pending-gst-returns-expiry-years.html); GSTN advisory on pending returns dated 10 Sep 2025 (per headline) — [Taxo](https://taxo.online/latest-news/10-09-2025-gstn-issued-advisory-to-file-pending-returns-before-expiry-of-three-years-effective-from-01-10-2025/). The TaxGuru summary says it does not cite subsections 37(3)/39(9); the article's effective date and the Taxo headline's effective date differ (1 Aug 2025 vs 1 Oct 2025 headline) — conflict, not resolved.
- Rule 80 and s.44 as the basis for filing 9/9C by 31 Dec — [CAclubindia](https://www.caclubindia.com/news/gst-portal-enables-gstr-9-and-gstr-9c-filing-for-fy-2025-26-26861.asp); [A2Z Taxcorp](https://a2ztaxcorp.net/gstr-9-gstr-9c-filing-enabled-for-fy-2025-26-on-gst-portal/)
- Reporting rule: RCM and ITC are reported in the year of payment/availment, not accrual (e.g., RCM paid in FY 2025-26 for FY 2024-25 goes in the FY 2025-26 GSTR-9) — [Taxscan, GSTN FAQs 5 Dec 2025](https://www.taxscan.in/gst/gstn-issues-additional-faqs-for-gstr-9-and-gstr-9c-compliance-1439380)
- GST rate change effective 22 Sep 2025 means FY 2025-26 spans two rate regimes — [NBA Office](https://www.nbaoffice.com/gstr-9-gstr-9c-fy-2025-26/); [A2Z Taxcorp](https://a2ztaxcorp.net/gstr-9-gstr-9c-filing-enabled-for-fy-2025-26-on-gst-portal/) (the latter cites "Notifications 9/2025 and 15/2025 (Rate)" — number plausibility unverified)

### Inferences
- Time limit for GSTR-1/3B amendments: s.37(3) and s.39(9) allow rectification of errors up to the earlier of 30 Nov following FY end or annual return date — **[UNVERIFIED]**, background knowledge; no fetched source.
