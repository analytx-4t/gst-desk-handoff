MODE 1: FY 2025-26 YEAR-END VETTING (GSTR-9 / 9C)
=====================
Context: the year is over and all monthly compliances (GSTR-1, 3B, RCM, ITC, DRC-03 etc.) are already filed. My team now needs to prepare and vet GSTR-9 and 9C strictly on the basis of what was actually filed, not what should have been filed.

Method:
1. Treat filed returns as the base. Build the annual position from the 12 months of GSTR-1, GSTR-3B (including Table 3.1, 3.1.1, 3.2, 4A-4D, 5), GSTR-2B and IMS action status. Then compare it with books (trial balance, sales and purchase registers, RCM ledger, ITC ledger), e-invoice and e-way bill data, and 26AS/AIS.
2. Prepare GSTR-9 table-wise (4 to 19) from filed data. Flag every place where books differ from filed returns.
3. Prepare the 9C reconciliation (turnover, tax paid, ITC) from the audited or final financials, with every reconciling item supported.
4. Run the full Departmental Risk Scan (Step 4 of the earlier prompt). Add these checks: Section 16(4) time limit for FY 2025-26 invoices, Rule 37 (180-day payment) reversals, Rule 37A (supplier non-payment) status, Rule 42/43, blocked credit under Section 17(5), and ITC claimed against suppliers who were cancelled or non-filing.
5. For every gap, classify:
   (a) Can still be corrected in GSTR-9 Table 10-14 (declare in the next-year return within the allowed cut-off)
   (b) Needs voluntary payment via DRC-03 before filing 9 (state tax, interest, and any late fee)
   (c) Cannot be corrected, so document support and defence
   (d) Needs ITC reversal, or reclaim where allowed
6. Compute exposure under Section 74A (applicable for FY 2024-25 onwards): tax, interest (Section 50), and the penalty the department could raise if the issue is found at audit or scrutiny. Show the effect of voluntary payment versus waiting for a notice.
7. Show the late fee exposure, with the due date applicable for FY 2025-26 as per the latest notification. If you are not sure of the due date, say so and ask me to confirm.

Mode 1 outputs: executive summary with risk rating, GSTR-9 working, 9C reconciliation, exceptions and risk register, DRC-03 computation, supporting-document checklist, draft explanation notes, open points.

=====================
MODE 2: FY 2026-27 ONGOING MONTHLY COMPLIANCE (April 2026 onwards)
=====================
Context: the team enters data month by month. The purpose is to catch errors early so that GSTR-9 and 9C for FY 2026-27 are clean and need no year-end fixing.

Each month the user enters: sales register, purchase register, GSTR-1 as filed, GSTR-2B, GSTR-3B (draft or filed), RCM working, ITC reversal working, e-invoice data, and payments made.

For each month, the tool must:
1. Reconcile: books vs GSTR-1 vs 3B vs e-invoice; 2B vs 3B ITC; RCM expense vs RCM liability paid.
2. Check ITC: eligibility, blocked credit, Rule 36(4)/IMS position, Rule 37, Rule 42/43, and invoices approaching the Section 16(4) deadline.
3. Check liability: tax paid in cash versus ITC, interest on late payment, and late fee on delayed returns.
4. Flag corrections to make in the next month's return (amendments, credit notes under Section 34 with their time limits, ITC reversals) before they pile up.
5. Maintain a cumulative April-to-date tracker (turnover, tax, ITC, RCM, reversals, exceptions) so the annual tables 4 to 9 are pre-built at year-end.
6. At each month-end, produce: (a) a monthly compliance report with a traffic-light status (Green/Amber/Red), (b) a list of actions before the due date, (c) a running "9/9C readiness" score and the open issues list.

Mode 2 outputs: monthly report, action list for the team, cumulative tracker, and open issues carried forward.
